Coastal regions and reference ports

Each region uses the tide harmonics of its reference port, calibrated against the Instituto Hidrográfico 2026 tide tables.

  • Tides of Norte — reference port: Leixões
  • Tides of Centro — reference port: Aveiro
  • Tides of Lisboa e Vale do Tejo — reference port: Lisboa
  • Tides of Alentejo — reference port: Sines
  • Tides of Algarve — reference port: Faro
  • Tides of Madeira — reference port: Funchal
  • Tides of Açores — reference port: Ponta Delgada

How the predictions work

Tide height is computed as a sum of harmonic constituents (M2, S2, N2, K2, K1, O1) with astronomical equilibrium arguments and nodal corrections from the 18.6-year lunar cycle. Because the constants are permanent properties of each port, predictions are valid for any date from August 2026 onwards, not just the calibration month.

Frequently asked questions

How are the tide predictions computed?

Using the harmonic method with six constituents (M2, S2, N2, K2, K1, O1), least-squares calibrated against the official Instituto Hidrográfico tide tables. All computations run locally in your browser.

Are the predictions valid for navigation?

No. The predictions are informational and do not replace the official Instituto Hidrográfico tide tables. They are not valid for navigation purposes.

Which regions have tide predictions?

All seven coastal regions: Norte, Centro, Lisboa e Vale do Tejo, Alentejo, Algarve, Madeira and Açores.
